# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router) + React 19 + Tailwind v4, already scaffolded. Animation libraries delegated: motion (Framer Motion), GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Lenis smooth scroll. Front-end demo only — no backend; forms and data are mocked.

## Users

People in Faiyum (and greater Egypt) shopping for a near-new used car — mostly SUVs and sedans like Kia Sportage, Hyundai Elantra. They currently discover stock through Facebook posts, then call or WhatsApp a salesperson. They ask about price and installments (قسط) in comments.

Secondary audience for this demo: the dealership owners (the potential client), who must recognize their brand and see it elevated.

## Product Purpose

A pitch/demo website for **دوحه موتورز — AUTO DOHA**, a used-car showroom in Faiyum. It shows current stock with full specs and photos, lets visitors estimate installments, compare cars, submit their own car for trade-in, and reach a salesperson directly. Success: the owners want to buy it.

## Positioning

"مملكة كسر الزيرو الحقيقي" — the kingdom of genuinely like-new (barely used) cars. Every car is shown with low mileage, full service history, new tires, protective film still on. Tagline: "اسم له تاريخ" (a name with history). Sign-off: "دوحة موتورز الاختيار الأنسب" (Doha Motors, the right choice).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Arabic RTL first, English toggle.
- Pages: home, inventory (filterable), car detail (gallery, specs, installment calculator), compare, sell/trade-in.
- Contact via WhatsApp/call links per salesperson.
- Prices are not published on their Facebook posts; demo prices are illustrative and must be labelled as such.

## Product Principles

1. Their own showroom and cars are the hero; stock photos only fill out the inventory.
2. Every car page answers the Facebook comment questions up front: price, installments, mileage, condition.
3. One tap to a human — salespeople are the real conversion path.
4. Arabic is the native reading direction, not a translation.
