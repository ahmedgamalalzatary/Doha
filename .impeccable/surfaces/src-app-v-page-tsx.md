Scope: AUTO DOHA demo site, three parallel visual directions under /faiyum, /showroom, /classic, switchable by a floating version switcher. Mode: Persuade. User will pick one after seeing all three.

Audience/job: Faiyum car shoppers on phones; find a like-new car, see price + installments, call/WhatsApp a salesperson. Owners must recognise their brand.

## Direction contract

### A — Faiyum: sand to lake (assigned, seed d3c0a336, candidate 7 of 7)
THESIS: the dealership as a piece of Faiyum; refuses the black-and-gold luxury dealer page.
OWN-WORLD: light = Wadi El Rayan ochre sand fields, dark = Lake Qarun night teal; red Ruqaa calligraphy; Alexandria type; banded dune-strata sections; the sāqiya waterwheel as the logo's wheel.
STORY: visitor sees real cars turning on the wheel, believes they are near-new and local, calls or opens a car.
FIRST VIEWPORT: giant waterwheel rising from the lower corner carrying car "buckets"; the top bucket's car is featured with name, km, price and "view car" + call; Ruqaa tagline large.
FORM: rotary selector + strata; raises: paint-colour re-tint on detail (generative identity), plate-coded dense inventory grid (convention catalog), banded courses (clay tower).
### B — Showroom light-lines (pick)
White stone + linear LED ceiling; light lines draw across photos; gold DOHA plate is the call button.
### C — Classic premium dealer (canon)
Hero photo + search bar, grid, financing band, played straight.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
